Things that affect how to read the numbers:
- From 1 to 4 workers the server's cores are saturated (92 to 97% busy) and the load
  generator is not: those rows measure the servers. At 8 workers the servers are 78
  to 96% busy and the load generator 69 to 81%.
- At 16 workers every server levels off at 2.28M to 2.53M requests/s with neither
  side saturated (server CPUs 52 to 94% busy, load generator 71 to 82%), and the
  median p99 goes above 100 ms for four of the five servers (Tokio stays at 1.7 ms).
  Under 6.8 the same servers reached 3.06M to 3.29M at 16 workers. 16 workers also
  share cores (SMT siblings), as before.
- Compared with the 6.8 run, every server is slower from 4 workers on, Tokio included,
  which does not use io_uring (tokio-raw at 4 workers: 1.36M here, 1.66M under 6.8),
  and the CPU time per request is higher. Since it hits epoll and io_uring servers
  alike, it comes from the kernel or the network driver rather than the runtimes. It
  was not investigated. Compare runtimes within one run, not across the two runs.
- run/, scale/, tls/ and fs/ ran on br-1 alone over loopback, as under 6.8.
