Spin governor behind spin_before_park (feat/spin-governor), measured with bench/spin.sh.

fixed = bench/giong built from origin/main (5a4cdb3): spin_before_park(200 us) spins
        200 us every time.
tuned = this branch: the same knob, the spin per worker doubles after an idle time
        <= 200 us and halves after one > 400 us.
none  = no spin.

Machine: Firecracker guest, Linux 6.18.44, 4 vCPUs Xeon 2.10 GHz, no cpuidle driver.
Server on CPU 0, 1 worker; load (bench/spin/src/bin/spin-load.rs, constant interval,
open loop) on the other CPUs. 5 rounds x 10 s per case; raw-busy.csv and
raw-blocking.csv hold every round, the tables below are medians over rounds.

What it shows
- CPU: up to 2,000 requests/s on one connection the governor costs what no spin costs
  (2.9-5.8% of a core against 22-44% for the fixed spin). From 5,000 requests/s it
  spins like the fixed one.
- p99, where it saves CPU: it has the latency of no spin, because it does not spin.
  With the busy client that is 13% (1,000/s) and 32% (2,000/s) above the fixed spin;
  with the blocking client it is +13% and -3%. At 2,000/s the requests come 500 us
  apart, so the fixed 200 us spin never catches one: what it buys is a shorter sleep
  afterwards (about 250 us instead of 450 us), and in this guest a shorter sleep wakes
  faster. That gain only comes with the CPU it burns, so no governor that saves the
  CPU can keep it here.
- p99, where it spins: equal or better with 50 connections, but worse at 5,000/s on
  one connection with the blocking client (160 against 82 us, same CPU) and at
  20,000/s with the busy client (157 against 90 us). Every round-to-round spread is
  wide (fixed alone: 61-280 us at 5,000/s), and an earlier 8-round run had the
  governor within 5% of the fixed spin at 1,000-2,000/s; this run does not.

Verdict: it does not meet "cut idle CPU without raising p99" against the fixed spin on
this machine. Not shipped; PR #186 closed with this data. A bare-metal run (no
hypervisor halt-polling) is what would show whether the low-rate p99 gap is the
guest's wake-up or the governor.
