# GIONG-E1402: write after shutdown

Raised by giong-compat, on `Compat` writes (`poll_write`, `poll_write_vectored`). In code: `giong::error::Code::CompatShutDown`.

## What happened

The sending direction of the stream was already shut down, by `AsyncWriteExt::shutdown` (Tokio) or `AsyncWriteExt::close` (futures-io), or by a library that calls them when it finishes, such as a TLS or WebSocket close. Nothing more can be sent on this stream. Reading still works until the peer closes its side.

## How to fix it

- Shut down only after the last write. If a library closes the stream for you, do not write to the stream after handing it over.
- To keep sending, open a new connection.
