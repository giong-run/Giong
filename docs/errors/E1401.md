# GIONG-E1401: an earlier buffered write failed

Raised by giong-compat, on `Compat` writes, flushes and shutdowns (`poll_write`, `poll_flush`, `poll_shutdown`, `poll_close`). In code: `giong::error::Code::CompatWriteFailed`.

## What happened

`Compat` buffers what is written through the Tokio or futures-io traits and sends it with giong's own `write_all`, the way `BufWriter` does. One of those sends failed, for example because the peer reset the connection. The first call after the failure returned the error from the kernel itself (such as `ConnectionReset` or `BrokenPipe`); every later write, flush or shutdown returns this code, with the kernel's error in the message. Bytes accepted since the last flush that succeeded may not have reached the peer.

Reading is not affected: whatever the peer sent before the failure can still be read.

## How to fix it

- Treat the connection as broken: drop it and open a new one. Resend what the protocol says must be resent.
- Call `flush` (`AsyncWriteExt::flush`) at the points where you need to know that the data was sent; a failure then shows up there and not at a later write.
